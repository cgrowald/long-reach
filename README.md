# Uprising (working title)

A reluctant parent in a generated country builds an underground movement from nothing: a laptop, a workbench, a few people they trust, then cells, armed groups, and an army that storms the capital.

Design: [docs/GAME_DESIGN.md](docs/GAME_DESIGN.md). The opening on paper: [docs/FIRST_WEEK.md](docs/FIRST_WEEK.md). The laptop: [docs/TERMINAL.md](docs/TERMINAL.md).

## Current build: T1, the laptop on its own
Godot 4.7. Open the project and press Play (main scene `terminal/terminal.tscn`).

- Four nights (Monday to Thursday) at the laptop in your garage, with short story cards in between.
- Apps: Messages, NetState browser, Files, Field Clearance, and something hidden.
- Things on the bench: Pavel's notebook, a sticky note (if you write your password down), the charger, and the lid.
- F9 shows a debug overlay (exposure, flags, secrets).

The old counter-terror prototype (tactical CQB, campaign map, enemy network) is still in the repo (`tactical/`, `world/`, `hq/`, `people/`) and will be reused; its design is in `docs/archive/`.
