## 2. The rules this adds up to (a draft for the user to correct)

1. **Our people: scrappy, mismatched, but one family.** Each fighter has their own silhouette (a puffer, a greatcoat, a work jacket, a blanket poncho), plus captured regime pieces and handmade rigs. What ties them together is the repairs and one shared mark (Q1).
2. **The regime: uniform, grey and new.** Clean slate and grey, plastic cards, scanners, cameras. New technology belongs to the regime.
3. **The people: old-world.** Paper, analog, repaired, warm. The garage is the people's world. HEARTH OS is the regime's world leaking in.
4. **Colour is resistance.** The regime took colour away. Our repairs, patches and stripes bring it back, and the more the movement grows, the more colour shows (Q1).
5. **Documents are clean and readable first, aged second.** Papers, Please clarity: a bold header, a few fields, one stamp. Wear is a light touch, not WW2 sepia.
6. **Night means pools of light in the dark** (This War of Mine), and the town is stairs, blank walls and doorways (the Casbah).
7. **Screens are physical:** a paper map with markers and a radio log (Radio Commander); knobs and readouts on worn plates (HighFleet).
8. **Shops and shelves have gear everywhere, but scrappy** (Luthen's shop without the polish).

## 3. Questions with options

**L1. What marks our people as one movement?**
- a) Colourful repairs: patches in colours the regime took (from 09 and 21). Every fighter's gear gets more colour as it gets repaired.
- b) One stripe: a red woven stripe, like the Andor blankets (the market tote already has a cream one).
- c) An armband in the movement's colour.
- d) Each district has its own patch colour, and the whole army is a quilt.
- e) The movement's symbol, painted or sewn on (we'd need to design one).
- f) a+b: colour from the repairs, and one red stripe as the badge.

**L2. How futuristic?**
- a) The regime has 2040s tech (plastic chip IDs, scanners, drones, CCTV everywhere); the people live in the 1980s (paper, CRTs, analog radios). The gap is the story.
- b) Everything is near-future, but cheap and worn on the people's side.
- c) Mostly analog, with a few chilling regime gadgets.

**L3. The ID card.**
- a) A regime plastic card: chip, laser photo, a hologram seal, an ID number, a district. Forgeries are laminated paper with a printed "chip".
- b) A plastic card plus a paper "residence slip" stapled to it (old meets new).
- c) A digital ID on a state phone (more future, less to hold).

**L4. Documents style.**
- a) Clean printed regime forms (a sans header, ruled fields, a red or blue stamp), lightly worn. Handwritten notes from people on top.
- b) GPT's aged-paper look, but cleaner.
- c) Mixed by source: regime forms crisp, people's papers handwritten and worn.

**L5. The regime's colours and uniform** (what captured gear looks like on our people).
- a) Slate grey and dark blue, with white piping.
- b) Olive drab and black (classic, closer to real armies).
- c) Charcoal with one sharp accent colour, like the hearth flame orange from HEARTH OS.
- d) Grey-green with reflective strips (a riot-police feel).

**L6. The gear cage, Battle Brothers style.**
- a) The 3D fighter in the middle, the equipped slots around them, the 2D stash grid right, the roster row along the bottom.
- b) As a), plus Battle Brothers' stat column under the fighter.
- c) As a), plus the history line from RESEARCH_IDEAS (the ops this rig went on).

**L7. Bench repairs, Pacific Drive style.**
- a) An outline overlay on the damaged part, a condition %, and a hands-on step (patch, re-tape, re-solder) with a tool bar at the bottom.
- b) Just a condition bar and a "repair" button.
- c) a, plus the repair leaves a visible patch in a movement colour (L1).

**L8. The curfew map's look.**
- a) Keep it top-down, restyled: pools of lamplight, black blocks, stairs and narrow doorways.
- b) Side-on cutaway like This War of Mine.
- c) Low-poly 3D streets in our pixel look (the biggest job).

**L9. Order for the face-lift.**
- a) Our people first (clothes, mismatch, colour) with the depot slice, since they're on screen in the fight.
- b) Documents first (file viewer, ID, ration card) with the intel work.
- c) The cage first (Battle Brothers layout).
- d) Bench repairs first (Pacific Drive).
- e) Each piece alongside whatever system it belongs to.
