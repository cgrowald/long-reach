# The art bible (user, 2026-11-23)

> "Concept art that will be our bible... the game HAS TO LOOK LIKE THIS AT SOME POINT OR WE ARE FAILING."

The images are in `docs/refs/bible/` (GPT-made, picked by the user). Other references (docs/LOOK_BIBLE.md, docs/refs/look/) still count, but this sheet is the target. Every face-lift piece is checked against it.

## What the images say

**The look**
- Low-poly faceted models with a woven, cross-stitch pixel texture on everything: cloth reads as fabric, even at pixel scale.
- Warm amber lamp pools against blue-black night.

**Our people (01, 16, 19)**
- Mismatched work clothes: denim, a brown greatcoat, a green work jacket, cargo trousers, boots.
- **Colourful stitched patches** everywhere (blue, mustard, wine, sage, lilac). **Two red stripes on the shoulder** are the movement's mark.
- Armbands are optional.
- Districts get their own palettes:
  - Mill Housing: teal denim.
  - Old Tram: wine and cream.
  - Canal Estate: brown wool.
  - Hill Steps: green and plum.

**Wear (06, 14)**
- Three states: **sound, worn, mended.**
- Repair is hands-on, with an outline round the damage and "CONDITION 63%".

**Starter kit (04)**
- A patched cap, fingerless gloves, a canvas harness, a shirt, trousers and boots.
- Each is a separate item that shows on the fighter.

**The regime (03, 07, 08, 11)**

| Tier | Look |
|---|---|
| Depot guard | grey wool jacket, navy trousers, a navy cap, a name tape |
| Duty lead | navy greatcoat, a radio box on the belt |
| Flame unit | black tunic, an orange collar trim, the flame badge, a belt box with a light |
| Honour guard | black greatcoat, orange lapels, the flame badge, visor glasses |

- The issued coat is clean and sharp. When we take it, it becomes the "reclaimed" coat: patched and frayed, with our stripe (02).

**Papers (13)**
- The Bureau side: a black plastic ID card with a chip and a hologram, crisp service forms, a dark desk.
- The people's side: a laminated paper ID, a yellow "PLEASE WAIT 27" ticket, a scribbled shopping list, a chipped enamel mug, a checked cloth.

**The depot (05, 10, 11, 18)**
- Dobrev Freight: cream brick, a navy wall band, the navy slash logo.
- Three kinds: parcel depot, bulk depot, cold store.
- Stores: cartons, blankets, gloves, cinder blocks, an olive NEMRA crate, scrap bins, ledgers, tags, a press tool, and blueprint boxes.
- **The vault: three numbered steel doors (01 02 03), with cables running along the wall to a control terminal.**

**The van (15)**
- A "HELMET · LIVE" CRT with the fighter's face inset, a "BACKUP 01:18" clock, and a PATCHBAY screen of **glyph symbols** (no hex codes).
- A radio, an enamel mug, a paper map. The monitors are branded VELTIN.

**The gear cage (17)**
- The fighter in 3D in the middle, slot icons connected by lines (shirt, pack, pouch, gloves, trousers, boots), the stash grid on the right, and portrait tiles along the bottom.

**The town (09)**
- Top-down, warm windows, awnings, stairs, arches, a regime scanner post with a light, a crossing, a parked car.

**Brands in the world**
- Talar Cola, Veltin (screens), Veska (boxes), Dobrev Freight, Nemra (crates).
- The movement's banner is a city skyline under an arch, in red on cream.
