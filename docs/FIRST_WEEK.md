# The first week: a play-by-play

*A paper playtest of one possible campaign start (2026-11-20). Everything is generated. These names are one example run. The point is to see whether the loop holds up before we build it.*

---

## Setup: character creation (about 5 minutes)

- **Country:** the Republic of **Valdenmark**, a fictional country. Ruled for 11 years by the **Council for National Renewal** (a military junta) under **Marshal Iosif Darvan** (personality: *paranoid*).
- **Your town:** **Kessel**, a mid-sized industrial town in the eastern province.
- **You:** *Tomas Halden*, 38.
  - **Grew up:** son of a railway worker.
  - **Job:** factory mechanic at the state truck plant.
  - **Family:** your wife *Mira* is a nurse; your daughter *Lena* is 9; your mother *Agnes* lives with you.
  - **What was taken:** your younger brother **Pavel**. Three months ago he went through the checkpoint on the river bridge and never came home. The police say there is no record of him.
  - **Temperament:** cautious.
- **Ideology:** "decide later" (it unlocks once the movement is big enough to need one).
- **Your circle** (generated from your backstory):
  - **Viktor**, your oldest friend, a police sergeant. Loves the country, believes in order, owes you for fixing his car for free for years.
  - **Hana**, a coworker at the plant: quiet, angry, her father was fired for joining a strike.
  - **Old Bruno**, your neighbor: nosy, retired, watches the street from his window.
  - **Dmitri**, your cousin, a conscript at the barracks outside town.
  - **Sela**, Mira's friend, runs the internet café.
  - **Father Anton** at St. Brigid's, who buried half the street.
  - **Ruslan**, a man at the market who "can find things".
  - **Your shift boss Koval**, a Party member.

The camera fades in on **the garage at night**. You're sitting at the old workbench. A **secondhand laptop** you bought for Lena's homework sits next to a box of Pavel's things the police finally gave back.

---

## Day 1 (Monday): the box

**Night (the game starts here; the tutorial is the story):**
- You open Pavel's box: a jacket, keys, a cracked phone, a **notebook with a phone number and the word "lantern"**.
- You open the laptop. It boots slowly, the fan whines, and the battery shows 41%. The desktop wallpaper is still the previous owner's dog. (*Quirk: whoever owned this laptop before left things behind. See TERMINAL.md.*)
- The browser is **NetState**, the state portal, which comes preinstalled. You search for Pavel's name. *"No results. Searches are logged for your safety."* A small eye icon in the corner flickers.
  - **You learned:** the laptop is watched. **Exposure +1.**
- You type the number from the notebook into **Messages**. It goes unanswered. The game ends the night.

**Overnight:** nothing happens on the map yet. The map isn't unlocked; you don't have anything on it.

> **Feel check:** quiet and personal. The player learns the first rule (searches are logged) by being bitten by it, the Papers Please way.

---

## Day 2 (Tuesday): the job

**Morning (laptop):**
- **News** (state): *"Marshal Darvan opens new bridge in the capital."* A small headline: *"Bridge checkpoint in Kessel to remain in place indefinitely."*
- **New rule today:** *"Internet cafés must log customer ID."* It means nothing to you yet. It will.
- A reply in Messages from the "lantern" number: **"who gave you this number"**. You can answer:
  1. *the truth* (Pavel's brother);
  2. *lie*;
  3. *ignore it*.
  
  You tell the truth. They reply: **"don't use this laptop. sela's café. thursday."**

**Day (job: factory mechanic minigame, ~2 minutes):**
- Three trucks come in. You diagnose and fix them by hand (a tactile repair minigame).
- One is a **police van**. You can fix it properly, or "fix" it so it breaks down in a week. Sabotage risks a trace if Koval inspects. You fix it properly; you're cautious.
- **Earned:** pay. **Job standing** (cover) is up a little.

**Evening (home):**
- Dinner. Lena asks if Uncle Pavel is coming for her birthday on Sunday. Choices: *the truth*, *"maybe"*, *change the subject*.
- Mira notices the notebook on the table. *"Is that Pavel's?"* You can tell her what you're doing or keep her out of it. You keep her out of it: **she doesn't know** (a flag that drives hundreds of later events).

**Night (room):**
- The **workbench unlocks**. You find Pavel's cracked phone in the box. A repair minigame: open it, swap the screen with a part you brought home from the plant. It powers on: **a photo of the river checkpoint and a list of plate numbers.**
- New file in **Files**: *pavel_photos*.

> **Feel check:** the job, the family and the bench all show up in one day, each small. Every system teaches through the story.

---

## Day 3 (Wednesday): the neighbor

**Morning:**
- **News:** the underground isn't visible yet; you only have state news.
- **New rule:** *"Curfew 23:00–05:00 in Kessel."* Now the night phase has a clock: anything you do outside after 23:00 risks being stopped.

**Day:** job. Hana, at the next bench, says quietly: *"You're Pavel Halden's brother. He came to our meetings."* She invites you to **"a reading group"** Friday.

**Evening:**
- Old Bruno stops you at the gate: *"Saw your garage light on at two in the morning. Working late?"*
- First **suspicion** from your circle. Choices:
  - *joke it off*;
  - *bring him a bottle* (costs money, Bruno likes you more);
  - *tell him to mind his business* (Bruno's opinion of you drops: a seed for later).
  
  You bring a bottle.

**Night:**
- You look at the plate list from Pavel's phone. The bench lets you print the photo on Lena's printer. The laptop warns: **printers here embed a serial number** (new knowledge). You decide not to print.
- You post nothing. Cautious.

---

## Day 4 (Thursday): the café

**Morning:** **new rule:** *"Possession of unregistered encryption software is a criminal offense."*

**Day:** you **skip work**. Cover drops; Koval will ask. You go to Sela's café instead.

**At the café** (a small scene; the café computer is a second, public terminal):
- You have to show your ID (Tuesday's rule). **A trace is logged, linking you to this café.**
- On the café PC, a message is waiting. The lantern contact gives you:
  - an address for the **underground forum** (it can't be found by search; you have to type it);
  - a password: *the name of the river*.
- The forum: posts about disappearances, a list of names from the bridge checkpoint. **Pavel's name is on it.** Next to it: *"Transferred to Station 4, Grenz Road."* **He may be alive.**

**Evening:** Mira asks why the plant called the house. You lie: you felt sick. Her trust drops a little.

**Night:**
- On your laptop you **install a VPN** from the forum (it's illegal since this morning). It might be real, or a regime honeypot; the forum post had a lot of upvotes.
  - The game rolls hidden. You'll find out later.
- With the VPN running, the eye icon goes dim.

> **Feel check:** this is where it clicks. A goal (Pavel at Station 4), a risk you can't fully read (the VPN), and real costs of acting (cover, Mira's trust, the café ID trace).

---

## Day 5 (Friday): the reading group

**Morning:** **new rule:** *"Gatherings of more than 5 people require a permit."*

**Day:** back at work. Koval: *"Sick, Halden? Next time bring a note."* Cover warning.

**Evening:**
- The reading group in Hana's basement: 6 people, so it's illegal since this morning.
- You meet:
  - **Josef**, a student who writes well;
  - **Marta**, a bus driver with routes past Station 4;
  - **a quiet man named Leo**, whom nobody vouches for.
- **First recruiting:** the **Network** app unlocks. You can ask people to help with *"something about Pavel"*:
  - **Hana** and **Josef** say yes.
  - **Marta** asks for time.
  - **Leo** says yes very quickly.
- Vetting Leo would take days. Your first gamble is whether to trust him.

**Night:**
- Your first propaganda. Josef drafts a post, you edit it in the **Web** app's forum editor, and you post Pavel's checkpoint photo with the caption *"Where are they?"*
  - **Followers +40.**
  - Bridge district **sympathy** up.
  - **Exposure +2.** The photo has metadata. Did you strip it? The Files app has a metadata tool, but only if you thought to look. (*A learnable angle.*)

---

## Day 6 (Saturday): the visit

**Morning:**
- **News** (state): *"Foreign-funded provocateurs spread fake photos of the river checkpoint."* They noticed.
- **Underground news** (the forum): your post was shared 2,000 times.
- **New rule:** *"Checkpoint on the river bridge now checks phones."*

**Day:** no work on Saturday. Family day.
- Lena's birthday is tomorrow. Choices: *buy the present* (time and money) or *work on the cause* (Mira notices).
- You buy the present.

**Evening: the knock.**
- **Viktor**, your cop friend, comes by with beer, off duty. He's worried: *"There's a file going around the station about Pavel's photo. They're looking at family."*
- He's warning you as a friend; he believes Pavel did something wrong. Choices:
  - *thank him*;
  - *ask what's in the file* (he'll tell you a little; his loyalty to you is tested);
  - *ask about Station 4* (he gets uneasy).
- While you talk, Lena comes into the garage looking for her bike. **The laptop is open on the bench** with the forum on screen. Do you close the lid in time? (*A quick-reaction moment in the room.*)

**Night:**
- A message from Marta: she's in. Her bus passes Station 4 twice a day. She can count the guards.
- A message from the lantern contact: **"leo is not who he says."**

---

## Day 7 (Sunday): the birthday

**Morning:** **new rule:** *"Rewards offered for information on online provocateurs."* Old Bruno reads the paper.

**Day:** Lena's birthday. A scene at home: cake, Agnes singing, Pavel's empty chair. Mira's hand on yours. No choices. Just the reason.

**Evening:** what do you do about Leo?
1. **Cut him out quietly.** He knows Hana's basement and your face.
2. **Feed him false information** and watch what comes back. That needs a channel he can't see, and patience.
3. **Confront him.** Dangerous.

**Night: the week summary.** This is the screen that's always waiting at the end of a week:
- **The movement:**
  - 4 people: you, Hana, Josef, Marta (and Leo?);
  - 1 safehouse-in-waiting (Hana's basement);
  - 40 followers.
- **Exposure:** you (low-medium), the café (logged), the bridge photo (being investigated).
- **Your family:** Mira's trust slightly down, Lena happy, cover warned once.
- **What you know:** Pavel may be alive in Station 4.
- **Grudges:** none yet. But Bruno saw the light, and Viktor saw your face when he mentioned the file.

**Week 2 opens:** the VPN was real, or wasn't. Viktor's station opens a file. Marta's guard count comes in. And Station 4 sits on the map, waiting for the first time you'll ask: *can we get him out?*

---

## What this paper test tells us

**What works:**
- **A personal goal right away** (Pavel) pulls the whole first week. It's generated from character creation, so it's different every run.
- **One new rule a day** gives the "new angles" feeling and teaches through consequences.
- **Every system shows up small:** job, home, bench, laptop, recruiting, propaganda, exposure. None of them overwhelms.
- **The room has drama:** the open laptop when Lena walks in.

**Risks to watch:**
- **Too much text:** the week is reading-heavy. The terminal and the bench have to be **hands-on toys**, not menus.
- **The day job can drag.** Keep it to ~2 minutes and make skipping it a real option with a cost.
- **Pacing:** the first 3 days are slow on purpose. They need atmosphere (sound, rain on the garage roof, the fan) to carry them.
- **Hidden rolls** (the VPN, Leo) must give the player **readable clues** so the outcomes feel earned, not random (Sigma Theory's mistake).

**What this needs from the terminal:** almost every key moment happens on the laptop:
- logged searches;
- the forum address you have to type;
- metadata in the photo;
- the illegal VPN;
- messages from the lantern;
- the eye icon.

So the terminal is the right first thing to build. See `TERMINAL.md`.
