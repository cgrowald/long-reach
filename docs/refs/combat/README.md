## Animation sheets (user, 2026-11-24): what our fighters' animations are based on
GPT-drawn, the same woman in the chest rig with the blue shoulder patch (the sewing table's look), smooth low-poly.
- **anim_walk_cycle.png:** CONTACT, PASS, CONTACT, SETTLE, IDLE. A relaxed walk with an arm swing, then settling into the idle.
- **anim_kneel_rise.png:** STAND, LOWER, KNEEL, RISE, STAND. Taking a knee (one knee down, hand on the other) and getting up.
- **anim_hold_fire.png:** HOLD, FLASH, SETTLE, HOLD. The blocky stylised gun held low at the hip; a muzzle flash; a small settle.
- **anim_over_shoulder_depot.png:** STAND, KNEEL, RELOAD, seen from behind at night outside the Dobrev depot (the third-person camera look).
- **anim_hit_flinch.png:** IDLE, FLINCH, STUMBLE, CATCH, RECOVER. Taking a hit: a twist away, a stumble, arms out for balance, back up.
- **anim_swap_a.png / anim_swap_b.png:** LOW, REACH, SWAP, RESET. Holding the gun low, a hand goes to the hip or rig, the swap (a reload or a part change), back to ready.
- **fx_bullet_impacts.png:** STREAK (tracer), FLASH (muzzle), PLASTER (dust puff and chips), METAL (sparks), WOOD (splinters and dust), FADE.

To be used when combat gets its pass (after the sewing table, user 2026-11-24). Together with the "fighters too jagged" note: the soldier gets rebuilt smoother toward these.
