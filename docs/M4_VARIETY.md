## 5. Built: V1, cover and bounding anywhere (2026-11-25)
- **v2/combat/cover.gd** (new): cover pieces from level data `cover: [{kind, pos, turn, len}]` (sandbags, barrier, car, crate, barrel, low_wall, booth). Each blocks walking, stops rounds (low pieces on physics layer 2: eyes see over, rounds stop; the booth on layer 1 like a wall) and makes cover spots round itself. `best(who, threat, reach, toward)` = the usual cover-shooter pick (the piece between you and the threat, near, not crowded, forward when bounding). Walking grid = Godot's stock **AStarGrid2D** with string-pulling, so people route round cover outside.
- **level.gd:** rooms can be `outdoor` (no walls of their own, no roof, a road floor, an apron of ground); `path(a, b)`, `is_outdoor`; doors refresh the grid when they open.
- **actor.gd:** `suppress` (rounds at you; you shoot worse and keep your head down), `ducked` (behind cover: unseen, can't shoot), `preaim` (up from cover already pointed where they last saw you), misses into cover puff on it, poorer troops via `acc_mult` / `slow` in the enemy data.
- **enemy_ai.gd:** in contact each one runs to cover, peeks and ducks (longer down than ours), hears gunfire 45 m outside, sees 30 m in daylight; **never moves up while your people are shooting**: only after 7 s of quiet, one at a time. A hit in cover shakes them half as much.
- **squad_ai.gd:** in contact anyone not busy with the doctrine takes cover and peeks/ducks; a MOVE to a point outside under fire (or a walk that gets shot at) becomes **bounding**: half runs to the next cover forward ("Moving!"), half shoots, "Set!", swap. A level can turn the room-by-room doctrine off (`doctrine: false`).
- **fight.gd / orders.gd / title:** the fight loads any level (`Game` meta `fight_level`); daylight levels get a sun and sky light; right-click "Move here" outside = that spot. Title: **Combat: roadblock (cover test)** (data/v2/combat/roadblock_test.json: Kessel Road, 5 conscripts, the bar's wall).
- Bot test (scratchpad bot_v1.gd): contact, both sides in cover, ducking, no enemy advance under fire, bounding swaps halves, fight over in ~40 s. Indoor depot tests re-run unchanged.
- Not done here (V2): the real first mission (politician, the bar chase, civilians, pick the way in and the hour), real models for the cover pieces, tuning.

## 7. Built: V3, the building generator (2026-11-25)
- **The pattern** (reuse, not invent): the standard procedural floor-plan recipe, *squarified treemap floor plans* (Marson & Musse 2010). Zones first (the rooms on the street, the rooms behind, maybe a wing out the back as an L), each zone split into rooms by floor-area weight with an ordered treemap, a hall only where the type wants one, then a spanning tree of doors that follows which rooms lead to which, plus a loop or two so there's always more than one way round. No download needed: it is a known algorithm, written straight into GDScript.
- **v2/sim/building_gen.gd** (sim, no nodes) + **data/v2/buildings.json** (all the numbers and words):
  - Six types: **bar, office, clinic, gun shop, police post, depot**. Each one sets its footprint size, the share on the street, its front rooms, back rooms, wing rooms (optional ones by chance), the hall style (none = rooms through rooms / cross = a hall behind the front rooms / spine = a hall down the middle), guards, people, weapons, the objective and two hours.
  - 28 room kinds: who links to whom (kitchen off the bar, records off the office), dead ends (washroom, cells, armory, the secure store), what's locked how often, open rooms that meet through wide openings with no door (bar room + snug, reception + waiting, warehouse + loading bay), dressing, guard and people weight.
  - Outside: the street in front (a parked car, junk), a side passage, an alley behind, a yard in the L or behind. A back door (a roller door on a loading bay), sometimes a side door. Lamps over the outside doors.
  - Guards on posts (patrols down halls and warehouse floors), people by room (drinkers, clerks, patients, prisoners in the cells), cameras in the big rooms, the objective spot.
  - The plan sheet comes with it: the two hours (busy: everyone in; quiet: fewer guards, no people, the front door locked) and a way in per outside door.
  - `check()` rejects a bad try (overlaps, rooms under 3 m, rooms over 3.4 to 1 long, a door off its wall, a room you can't reach, the objective room lost, only one way in) and the generator tries again. 360 test layouts: all clean, nearly all first try.
- **Seed = the building**: `gen:<type>:<seed>` as a level id always makes the same building, so the plan sheet, the fight and the Bureau form all agree.
- **New furniture** in art/depot_kit.glb (art/src/make_depot_kit.py): counter, bottle shelf, café table with chairs, clinic bed, gun rack, waiting-room bench, wrapped pallet.
- **level.gd:** wide openings (`arch` doors with a width), open ground that runs straight on into the next bit of open ground (no seams), room routes across open ground (street to yard) walk the grid round the building; furniture can stand free (`spot`).
- **The squad:** a building raid starts on the street (the street and yard count as cleared); inside, they never pick a back door outside over the next door inside.
- **The plan sheet** now has a pencil sketch of the place (rooms, doors, the ways in numbered like the cards, X = what you came for; no guards, that's what scouting is for in V4). On a generated building: tabs for the six types and ANOTHER ONE (R). Title: **Combat: generated building**.
- **The depot raid moved onto it** (G1): every raid is a fresh generated depot from the raid's seed (the secure store pinned at the back, the vault cabled to its PC, the alarm box in security, guard posts from the generator). The Bureau SERVICE FORM draws that night's depot.
- **Bot runs** (scratchpad bot_v3_fight.gd, the bot only ever goes in the front door and answers every door with "open"): 18 buildings across all six types, every one taken after tuning. First pass had two wipes (a bar and a gun shop: shotgun guards in a lit room at the door); guard aim was lowered per type and a building has at most one shotgun, so the enemy stays worse than you (user Q7). Police posts take 4-6 minutes. Depot raid from the garage (bots c5 freight/raid, d) passes on the generated depot.
- **Fixed on the way:** Kessel Road's "take him alive" no longer starts an objective grab when his room is cleared (that left a fighter flagged busy, so nobody cuffed the deputy in some runs). All five Kessel plans end with him taken.
- **Not yet:** guards are placed by room type, not scouted or shown (V4: hacking scouts the plan); generated buildings aren't linked to the garage or the map yet (V4/V5); no windows; one floor; gun shops are small (3-5 rooms); the sketch's room labels crowd in narrow rooms.
