# M0 playtest: what's already built (2026-11-23)

Play in any order. For each numbered step, note what happened if it wasn't what the "Expect" line says, and anything that felt bad, confusing or ugly. Write notes like "7: the gate lock never opened".

Handy: **F1** opens the debug panel. It has buttons to skip ahead: Open all markets, Learn all hacks, +100 cr, +1 night action, End day, +1 drop stick.

## Start
1. **Title → New game.**
   - Expect: three job cards (hospital, steel mill, textile mill) with photo placeholders, what each gives and costs. Hover lifts a card.
2. **Pick a job, then the morning card.**
   - Expect: a dark framed card.
   - From day 2: choose how much to steal (nothing, a little, a lot).

## The garage
3. **Look around with A / D.**
   - Expect: each bench has its own desk lamp with an orange glow; a hanging amber lamp; tape, a photo and a coffee ring on the desk.
4. **Click the med table, then make a Torn-shirt bandage by hand.**
   - Expect: the lamp brightens to an orange pool while you work.
   - Expect: amber brackets and callouts show where to act.
   - At the end: the formula rolls up into stars, and a tied cloth roll drops onto the bench and turns.
5. **The hacking bench: make a Port scanner.**
   - Expect: an olive gadget case appears on the bench at the end.
   - Expect: the upgraded case if you score 4 stars or more.
6. **The box on the desk:** drag the scanner into the SCAN slot.
7. **The shelves:**
   - Expect: the stash in columns, with grade dots on materials.
   - Expect: the gear cage shows your 3 people with faces and the 3D fighter.

## The PC and hacking
8. **Click the PC and log in.** The password is on the notes (hearth123).
   - Expect: a top bar, a left dock (Files, Mail, Jobs, Drop, Shop, Lantern, Tools, Shell).
9. **Jobs (Patchbay):**
   - Expect: only **Post Office No. 4**, with kettle's line and "LEARN IT HERE" next to GRAB, REROUTE and WIPE.
   - Press CONNECT and route the signal. Then do the code chain.
   - Expect: kettle's tips change at each stage. Try to run all three programs and jack out.
   - Expect: "You learned GRAB / REROUTE / WIPE". The hack costs 1 night action.
10. **Get cut out on purpose once** (wait for the trace to fill).
    - Expect: no lock on tutorial targets; you can just try again.
11. **Next night: the Town Hall** (teaches DIRT, EYES, PAYDAY).
    - Expect: after it, the normal town list appears.
12. **Drop app: sell some files to the fixer.**
    - Expect: at trust 3, a line about lantern://.
    - Expect: at trust 5, a line about the Tram Yard.
    - Also post one on Nod: followers go up.
13. **Shop (ShopNet): buy Salvaged chips.**
    - Expect: a "LOGGED" warning (Bureau attention up).
14. **Sleep (house door).**
    - Expect: the night ledger shows pay, upkeep, "Parcel arrived" and the rerouted crate.
15. **Lantern** (F1 → Open all markets, if you haven't got trust 3): buy something.
    - Expect: it arrives at the dead drop overnight (sometimes a scam).

## The curfew trip
16. **Roller door → GO OUT** (costs a night action).
    - Expect: a top-down night map with patrol torches, a searchlight, lamps and dark corners.
    - Controls: click to walk, hold Shift to run (loud).
17. **Click the padlocked gate** (orange, mid-right) to pick it.
    - Controls: move the mouse sideways to put the pick under a pin; hold left and move up to lift; mouse wheel or W/S for tension; Space lets go; Esc gives up.
    - Expect: the pick shakes as you near the sweet spot, a click when a pin sets, a snap if the tension is too high.
18. **Reach the Tram Yard (top right).**
    - Expect: 9 stalls with grades. Buy something.
    - Trade a material for credits (TRADE YOURS).
    - Watch for a too-cheap stall with a red "tell" line: it's a sting.
19. **HEAD HOME.**
    - Expect: getting home puts the bag on the shelves.
    - Get caught once on purpose: bribe, or let them search you (you lose the bag).

## Elsewhere
20. **Title → Lockpick toy.**
    - Expect: four locks, each harder; the last ones have pins that false-set (ease the tension off).
21. **Gear cage → put the rigs on → Send them.**
    - Expect: the depot raid with the FIELD LINK screen: faces per fighter, the cam feeds, the ops board, then the op report.
    - Look at the new rigs and pack on the fighters.
